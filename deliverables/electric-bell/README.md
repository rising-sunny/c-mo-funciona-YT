# Electric bell — episode 3

30 seconds, 1080×1920, native 60 fps. English expressive narration, short-form captions, equations, licensed music and original procedural gong sounds.

All arrows, field lines, current traces and explanatory callouts are independent transparent 2D graphics. They are not Blender meshes or part of the base 3D render.

The classic self-interrupting bell is shown with an illustrative lumped electromechanical model and expanded/replayed time. Coil current decays through a simplified transient path, the hammer carries inertia, and the spring returns the armature. This is not a measured or fitted commercial device. See docs/electric-bell.md in the repository for assumptions and checked sources.

Music credit is in MUSIC_CREDITS.txt and the episode description. No upload to YouTube or Facebook has been performed.
